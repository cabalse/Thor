Each side takes turns moving one piece per turn, starting with Alliance.
A piece may move into any area directly connected to its current area, up to a number of areas equal to its movement (m) stat, as long as every area along the path is connected to the next.
A piece cannot move through or into an area already occupied by two or more friendly pieces.
When a piece moves into an area occupied by an enemy piece, a battle occurs.
In a battle, the attacker's attack (a) stat is compared to the defender's defense (d) stat plus a bonus equal to half the defense value of the terrain the defender occupies, rounded down.
If the attacker's value is higher, the defending piece is removed from the board and the attacking piece occupies the area.
If the defender's value is higher or equal, the attacking piece is removed from the board and the defending piece remains in place.
Swamp terrain reduces the movement stat of any piece entering it by 1 for that turn, to a minimum of 1.
Mountain terrain adds 2 to the defense of any piece stationed there.
A side loses the game when it has no pieces remaining on the board.
The game also ends in a decisive victory for a side if that side controls every City-terrain area at the start of its turn.
